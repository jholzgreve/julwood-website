Privacy

This site is deliberately simple. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and has no forms.

Who is responsible

Julia Holzgreve, Julwood, Zurich, Switzerland. For any question about your data, write to julia@julwood.com.

Visiting this site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. When you open a page, GitHub's servers process your IP address and technical details of the request, such as the date, time and browser type, in order to deliver the page and keep the service secure. This means the data is transferred to the United States. GitHub states that it complies with the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks. I have no access to these logs and do not use them. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

The typefaces and images are served from this site. Nothing is loaded from other servers.

Writing to me

If you send me an email, I use your address and the content of your message to reply and to follow up on your enquiry. Email for julwood.com runs on Google Workspace, so messages are processed on Google's servers, which may be located outside Switzerland, including in the EU and the United States. I keep correspondence only as long as it is needed for that purpose.
