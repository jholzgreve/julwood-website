What I do

AI adoption & transformation

Most AI initiatives stall after the pilot, when legal, risk and middle management have to decide whether to trust the output. I help leadership teams through that last mile and with the organisation behind it: who owns AI, where the teams sit and how decisions are made.

Go-to-market strategy

For AI companies entering regulated markets. I help them choose where to start, shape an offer that banks and insurers can buy, and plan the route to their first enterprise clients.

Ecosystem partnerships

Some things only work together. I find the anchor partners for a new initiative in Swiss financial services, bring banks, insurers and AI companies to one table, and help run what they start.
