What I do

AI adoption

Most AI initiatives stall after the pilot, when legal, risk and middle management have to decide whether to trust the output. I help leadership teams through that last mile, in workshops and alongside the work.

Organising for AI

Who owns AI, where the teams sit and how decisions are made. Practical organisational design for banks and insurers that want AI to last beyond the first use cases.

Partnerships

For AI companies entering regulated markets. I help them find the right anchor partners in Swiss financial services and earn their trust.
